Our Heritage & Vision

Why Roots & Spice? Connecting Two Culinary Powerhouses

Founded in Germany, **Roots & Spice** was born from a passion to unite the rich agricultural heritage of the African continent with the legendary spice and flavor traditions of Asia. Both culinary cultures share a profound dedication to bold flavors, aromatic herbs, healing roots, and communal dining.

African Roots

Puna yams, plantain flour, egusi seeds, palm oil, and authentic West African peppers.

Asian Spices

Ceylon cinnamon, Szechuan peppercorns, fragrant basmati, matcha, and authentic curry pastes.

Based in Fürstenwalde

Serving local food enthusiasts and shipping across Germany with pride.
